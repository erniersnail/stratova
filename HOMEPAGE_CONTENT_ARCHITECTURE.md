## Emotional Journey

The visitor arrives with a question: *"Is this firm credible enough to trust with capital?"*

The homepage must answer that question not through claims, but through evidence of rigor, discipline, and transparency. The emotional arc is a slow build of trust — from curiosity to respect to confidence to action.

### Journey Map

| Stage | Emotion | What the visitor thinks | What the page does |
|-------|---------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Arrival** | Curious, skeptical | "Who are they? Why should I care?" | Hero establishes identity and positioning immediately. Clean, confident, no jargon. |
| **Orientation** | Evaluating | "Are they credible? Do they have a real process?" | Trust & Credibility section provides evidence of institutional quality. |
| **Engagement** | Interested | "How do they think? Does it align with my philosophy?" | Investment Philosophy articulates the intellectual framework. The visitor begins to see alignment. |
| **Exploration** | Intrigued | "What do they actually do? How does it work?" | Strategies Overview and Research Process show the machinery. Concrete enough to be believable, high-level enough to protect intellectual property. |
| **Validation** | Respectful | "Their approach is rigorous. Can I see results?" | Performance Overview (without numbers) signals they have a track record without making inappropriate claims. |
| **Deepening** | Confident | "They publish their research. This is a real research firm." | Research Library Preview demonstrates intellectual output. This is the strongest trust signal. |
| **Resolution** | Ready | "I want to learn more or take the next step." | FAQ resolves remaining objections. Newsletter and footer provide clear, low-friction next steps. |

### Emotional Arc Summary

**Skepticism → Curiosity → Intellectual respect → Confidence → Readiness to engage**

The page never demands trust. It earns it, section by section, through demonstrated rigor and transparency.

## Single Most Important Message

> **"Stratova Quant builds systematic investment strategies through disciplined, evidence-based research — not intuition, not hype, not luck."**

Every section of the homepage should reinforce this message. If a visitor remembers nothing else, they should remember that Stratova Quant is a research-first firm where process precedes outcome.

## Three Homepage Principles

Every future revision of this homepage must preserve these three principles:

### Principle 1: Evidence over Claims

No statement on the homepage should be unsupported. If the page says "rigorous research," the Research Process section must show what that means. If it says "disciplined," the Investment Philosophy must articulate the discipline. The page should feel like a prospectus, not a pitch deck.

### Principle 2: Transparency as a Differentiator

The homepage should reveal more than a typical asset manager's website. Show the research process. Show the thinking. Show the philosophy. The willingness to be transparent is itself a trust signal. The visitor should feel they understand *how* Stratova Quant thinks, not just *that* they think.

### Principle 3: Institutional Restraint

The homepage should look and read like it belongs to a firm managing institutional capital. No superlatives. No exclamation points. No claims of "best" or "leading." Every sentence should pass the test: *"Would this language appear in a communication from a respected institutional investment firm?"* If the answer is no, rewrite it.

## Section 1: Navigation

### Objective

Enable intuitive exploration of the site while reinforcing the firm's identity with every click.

### Why it exists

Navigation is the visitor's map. It must be predictable, minimal, and trustworthy. Every link should feel like a genuine invitation to learn more — not a trap leading to a sales pitch.

### Exact Headline (not applicable — navigation is link-based)

### Supporting Copy (link labels)

```
Home
Strategies
Research
About
Contact
```

### Information Hierarchy

```
[Stratova Quant logo / wordmark]          Home | Strategies | Research | About | Contact
```

- **Primary links:** Home, Strategies, Research, About, Contact
- **No dropdowns on desktop.** If sub-navigation is needed, use a separate page, not a mega-menu.
- **No CTA button in the navigation.** The firm does not need to "Get Started" or "Book a Call" from the nav. The navigation is for exploration, not conversion.
- **Sticky navigation** on scroll. Minimal height. Background matches page background.

### User Action

Click a link to explore the corresponding section or page. No pressure. No urgency. The visitor navigates when they are ready.

## Section 2: Hero

### Objective

Answer the question *"Who are you and what do you do?"* in under three seconds. Establish identity, positioning, and tone.

### Why it exists

The hero is the visitor's first impression. It must communicate that this is a serious, institutional investment research firm — not a fintech app, not a crypto platform, not a marketing website.

### Exact Headline

**Systematic investment research, built on evidence.**

### Supporting Copy

Stratova Quant develops disciplined, data-driven investment strategies for long-term investors. We cover United States equities and Indian equities — two of the world's most liquid and transparent markets.

*No subheadline. No CTA button. No tagline. The headline and the supporting paragraph are sufficient.*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Systematic investment research, built on evidence."
2. Supporting paragraph (as above)
```

- **No CTA button.** The visitor is not ready to act. They need to evaluate first.
- **No statistics, no "managing $X billion," no "X years of experience."**
- **No scrolling indicators, no arrows, no "learn more" prompts.**
- The hero is quiet, confident, and complete.

### User Action

Read. Pause. Decide whether to scroll further.

## Section 3: Trust & Credibility

### Objective

Provide immediate, verifiable signals of institutional quality without making unsupported claims.

### Why it exists

Skepticism is the default emotional state of a sophisticated investor evaluating a new firm. This section must answer the implicit question: *"Why should I take you seriously?"*

### Exact Headline

**Research-driven. Independent. Transparent.**

### Supporting Copy

Stratova Quant is an independent quantitative investment research firm. We are not a broker-dealer. We are not a fund manager. We are a research firm that develops systematic investment strategies grounded in academic rigor and empirical evidence.

Our research covers two of the world's most extensively studied equity markets: the United States and India. We focus on markets where data is available, liquid, and reliable — because research is only as good as the data it uses.

*No logos. No client logos. No awards. No media mentions. No "as featured in."*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Research-driven. Independent. Transparent."
2. Supporting paragraph (as above)
3. Three brief pillars (visual, not text-heavy):
   - Independent research
   - Evidence-based methodology
   - Institutional-grade transparency
```

- Each pillar is a short phrase followed by a single sentence — no more.
- The pillars should be visually organized (e.g., three columns) but the content is the focus.

### User Action

Absorb the three pillars. Begin to associate Stratova Quant with rigor and independence.

## Section 4: Investment Philosophy

### Objective

Articulate the intellectual framework that guides all research and strategy development at Stratova Quant.

### Why it exists

Sophisticated investors want to understand *how* a firm thinks before they evaluate *what* it produces. Philosophy is the foundation. Without it, the rest of the page has no context.

### Exact Headline

**Our philosophy**

### Supporting Copy

We believe that consistent investment outcomes are the result of disciplined process, not prediction.

Our approach is built on three principles:

**1. Evidence over intuition.**
We do not rely on gut feelings, market narratives, or macroeconomic forecasts. Every strategy we develop must be grounded in reproducible empirical evidence.

**2. Process over outcomes.**
We focus on building processes that are robust, transparent, and repeatable. We accept that individual outcomes are uncertain — but a disciplined process, maintained over time, produces reliable results.

**3. Transparency over opacity.**
We publish our research, explain our methodology, and share our thinking. We believe that the best way to earn trust is to be open about how we work.

*No jargon. No "alpha," "beta," "factor tilts," or "risk premia." The philosophy should be understandable to an educated non-specialist.*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Our philosophy"
2. Opening sentence: "We believe that consistent investment outcomes..."
3. Three principles, each with:
   - Principle name (bold)
   - One-sentence explanation
```

- The three principles are the core of this section. They should be visually distinct but not separated by heavy chrome.
- No illustrations, no icons, no decorative elements.

### User Action

Read and reflect. The visitor should feel intellectual alignment — or not. If they do not agree with the philosophy, they should self-select out. That is a feature, not a bug.

## Section 5: Strategies Overview

### Objective

Describe what Stratova Quant produces without over-promising or using technical jargon.

### Why it exists

After understanding the philosophy, the visitor needs to know what the firm actually *does*. This section provides a high-level overview of the strategies offered.

### Exact Headline

**Our strategies**

### Supporting Copy

We develop systematic investment strategies for two of the world's largest equity markets.

**United States Equities**
Our US equity research focuses on liquid, large-capitalization stocks. We develop strategies that seek to capture risk-adjusted returns through systematic exposure to empirically validated factors.

**Indian Equities**
Our India equity research applies the same rigorous methodology to one of the world's most dynamic emerging markets. We believe that disciplined process is even more valuable in markets where information asymmetry is higher.

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Our strategies"
2. Opening sentence: "We develop systematic investment strategies..."
3. Two market sections, each with:
   - Market name (e.g., "United States Equities")

```

- The two markets should be presented as equal in importance.
- No comparison between the two markets.
- The language should be specific enough to be informative, general enough to protect intellectual property.

### User Action

Understand the scope of the firm's research. Decide if the markets covered are relevant to their interests.

## Section 6: Research Process

### Objective

Demonstrate the rigor of the research methodology without revealing proprietary details.

### Why it exists

This is the section that separates Stratova Quant from marketing-driven asset managers. Showing *how* research is done — not just claiming it's rigorous — builds genuine trust.

### Exact Headline

**How we research**

### Supporting Copy

Every strategy we develop follows a structured research process.

**1. Hypothesis formation.**
We begin with a testable hypothesis grounded in academic literature or empirical observation. Every hypothesis must be falsifiable.

**2. Data analysis.**
We test hypotheses against historical data using statistical methods. We account for survivorship bias, look-ahead bias, and data mining concerns.

**3. Strategy development.**
Hypotheses that survive empirical testing are developed into systematic strategies with clearly defined rules, parameters, and risk constraints.

**4. Ongoing evaluation.**
Strategies are continuously monitored and evaluated. We publish our findings — both positive and negative — in our research library.

*No diagrams. No flowcharts. No "proprietary" claims. No "secret sauce."*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "How we research"
2. Opening sentence: "Every strategy we develop follows..."
3. Four steps, each with:
   - Step name (e.g., "Hypothesis formation")
   - 1–2 sentence explanation
```

- The steps should feel sequential but not rigid.
- The language should be concrete enough to be credible, but not so detailed that it exposes proprietary methodology.
- The final step ("Ongoing evaluation") is important — it signals that the firm is self-critical and transparent about results.

### User Action

Gain confidence in the intellectual rigor behind the strategies. The visitor should think: *"This is a real research firm, not a marketing operation."*

## Section 7: Performance Overview

### Objective

Signal that the firm has a track record without making inappropriate claims or presenting non-compliant performance data.

### Why it exists

Investors want to know that a firm has skin in the game and a history of execution. But presenting performance data on a public website — especially without regulatory disclosures — is inappropriate for an institutional research firm. This section acknowledges the question without attempting to answer it with numbers.

### Exact Headline

**Performance**

### Supporting Copy

We believe that performance should be evaluated in context — not through headlines or summary statistics.

Our strategies are developed through a disciplined research process and evaluated against rigorous benchmarks. We do not publish performance data on this website because context matters, and context requires a conversation.

If you are an institutional investor interested in understanding our track record, we invite you to reach out. We will share our performance history, our methodology, and our full research documentation.

*No numbers. No charts. No "since inception" returns. No Sharpe ratios. No drawdown figures. No comparison to benchmarks.*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Performance"
2. Supporting paragraph: "We believe that performance should be evaluated..."
3. Invitation to contact for detailed performance information
```

- This section is intentionally brief. The point is to acknowledge the question and redirect to a private, appropriate channel.
- No CTA button that says "View Performance" — that would be misleading. The CTA is a text link to the contact page.
- The language should be confident and transparent, not defensive.

### User Action

Understand that the firm has a track record but handles it appropriately. If the visitor is sufficiently interested, they will navigate to the contact page.

## Section 8: Research Library Preview

### Objective

Demonstrate intellectual output by showing that the firm publishes research. This is the strongest trust signal on the page.

### Why it exists

A firm that publishes research is a firm that is confident in its thinking. The research library is proof that Stratova Quant is a genuine research operation — not a marketing website pretending to be a research firm.

### Exact Headline

**Research library**

### Supporting Copy

We publish our research — including strategy documentation, market analyses, and methodology papers — in our research library.

Recent publications include:

- *Factor Exposure in Indian Equity Markets: An Empirical Analysis*
- *The Role of Volatility Regimes in Portfolio Construction*
- *Transaction Cost Analysis for Systematic Strategies in US Large-Cap Equities*

*Each title is a real, plausible research paper title. No dates. No "download" counts. No PDF icons.*

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Research library"
2. Supporting paragraph: "We publish our research..."
3. 3–4 research paper titles (as text links)
4. Link to full research library
```

- The paper titles should be substantive and specific. They should sound like academic working papers, not blog posts.
- Each title is a link to the full paper.
- A "View all research" link at the bottom leads to the full research library page.
- No call-to-action button. A text link is sufficient.

### User Action

Click a research paper title to read the full paper. The visitor should think: *"These are real papers. This is a real research firm."*

## Section 9: Frequently Asked Questions

### Objective

Address the most common questions and objections that prevent a sophisticated investor from taking the next step.

### Why it exists

FAQ sections are often afterthoughts. Here, they are strategic. The questions should be the actual questions that institutional investors ask when evaluating a research firm. The answers should be direct, informative, and transparent.

### Exact Headline

**Frequently asked questions**

### Supporting Copy

**Q: Is Stratova Quant a registered investment adviser?**

A: Stratova Quant is an independent quantitative investment research firm. We are not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or fund manager. We produce research that may be used by registered investment advisers and institutional investors as part of their investment process.

**Q: Do you manage client assets?**

A: No. We are a research firm, not an asset manager. We develop systematic investment strategies and publish our research. We do not manage discretionary accounts or accept client assets.

**Q: How do you generate revenue?**

A: We generate revenue through research subscriptions and institutional consulting engagements. We do not receive commissions, markups, or any form of transaction-based compensation.

**Q: How often do you publish research?**

A: We publish research on an ongoing basis. Publication frequency varies depending on the nature of the research. Our research library contains all current and historical publications.

**Q: Can individual investors access your research?**

A: Our research is primarily intended for institutional investors, registered investment advisers, and family offices. Individual investors interested in our research should contact us to discuss access options.

**Q: What markets do you cover?**

A: We currently cover United States equities and Indian equities. We focus on markets where data is liquid, transparent, and reliably available.

**Q: How can I evaluate your track record?**

A: We share detailed performance history and methodology documentation with qualified institutional investors through a confidential process. Please contact us to begin that conversation.

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Frequently asked questions"
2. 7 questions, each with:
   - Question (bold or visually distinct)
   - Direct answer (1–3 sentences)
```

- No accordion or expand/collapse interaction. All questions and answers are visible at once. Transparency means not hiding answers behind clicks.
- Questions are ordered by strategic importance, not alphabetically.
- Answers are direct, honest, and complete. No evasion. No "it depends."
- The tone is informative, not defensive.

### User Action

Read the question most relevant to their concern. Find a direct, honest answer. Move closer to taking action.

## Section 10: Newsletter

### Objective

Offer a low-friction way for visitors to stay connected with Stratova Quant's research.

### Why it exists

Not every visitor is ready to contact the firm. Some want to observe, evaluate, and learn over time. The newsletter is a way to stay on their radar without requiring a commitment.

### Exact Headline

**Stay informed**

### Supporting Copy

Receive our research, market analyses, and strategy documentation directly in your inbox. We publish when we have something to say — not on a fixed schedule.

No spam. No sales pitches. No market commentary for the sake of filling a calendar.

### Information Hierarchy

```
1. Headline: "Stay informed"
2. Supporting paragraph (as above)
3. Email input field (no CTA button — or a simple "Subscribe" label)
4. Privacy note: "We will never share your email address."
```

- The email input should be simple and unobtrusive. One field. No name, no company, no phone number.
- The "Subscribe" label should be understated — a text link or subtle button, not a prominent CTA.
- The privacy note is mandatory. It builds trust.
- No promise of frequency. No "weekly newsletter" or "monthly digest." The firm publishes when it has something to say.

### User Action

Enter an email address. Receive research over time. The visitor remains in the consideration set without needing to make a decision today.

## Appendix: Content Rules

1. **No exclamation points.** None. The tone is institutional and measured.
2. **No superlatives.** No "best," "leading," "top," "premier," "unmatched."
3. **No first-person plural in marketing contexts.** "We believe" is acceptable in the philosophy section. "We are the best" is not.
4. **No claims that require proof the page does not provide.** If the page says "rigorous," the process section must demonstrate rigor.
5. **No urgency.** No "limited time," "act now," "subscribe today."
6. **No guarantees.** No "consistent returns," "risk-adjusted," "preserve capital."
7. **No data that requires regulatory disclosure.** No performance numbers, no AUM, no client counts.
8. **Every section serves a purpose.** If a section does not advance the emotional journey, remove it.
9. **The page should work without images.** If the content is removed from its visual context, it should still be comprehensible and compelling.
10. **The page should be printable.** A visitor should be able to print the homepage and hand it to a colleague. It should read like a well-written investment memorandum.
