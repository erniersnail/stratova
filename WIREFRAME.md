## Structural Notes

### Page Dimensions
- **Page width:** 1440px
- **Content width:** 1200px (centered within page)
- **Vertical spacing between sections:** Approximately 120px–160px of whitespace
- Each section is bounded by generous padding above and below

### Section Flow
1. **Navigation** — Fixed/sticky, transparent background, minimal height
2. **Hero** — Full viewport height minus nav, vertically centered content
3. **Trust Section** — Three equal-width cards, horizontally distributed
4. **Investment Philosophy** — Two-column layout (headline left, principles right)
5. **Strategies** — Two large cards stacked vertically
6. **Research Process** — Horizontal timeline with five connected nodes
7. **Performance Preview** — Two-column layout (text left, chart placeholder right)
8. **Research Library** — Three equal-width paper cards in a row
9. **Newsletter** — Centered, single-column layout
10. **Footer** — Four-column layout with copyright bar

### Whitespace Strategy
- 80px–120px padding between each major section
- 40px–60px padding within sections between content elements
- 24px–32px padding inside cards and containers
- The hero section should feel particularly open — no content below the fold for the first viewport

### Typography Direction (for reference)
- **Hero headline:** 48px–56px, light weight
- **Section headings:** 24px–30px, medium weight
- **Body text:** 16px, regular weight
- **Card text:** 14px–15px, regular weight
- **Small labels:** 12px–13px

### Interactive Elements
- Primary buttons: "Explore Research," "Subscribe"
- Secondary buttons: "Our Philosophy"
- Text links: "Learn More →," "Read Article →," "View all research →"
- Email input field in newsletter section
- All interactive elements are underlined or visually distinct on hover

### What This Wireframe Does Not Include
- No advertisements
- No testimonials
- No logos (client or partner)
- No awards, badges, or certifications
- No counters, progress bars, or statistics
- No client numbers or AUM figures
- No media mentions or press logos
- No pricing information
- No fabricated data of any kind
- No images, illustrations, or photographs
- No social media icons or feeds
